Who we are

A fastener house run by fastener people.

YOU-ONE Fastening Systems was established in 2008 as a Korean manufacturer and exporter of fasteners, fastening tools and equipment and fastener collating materials — and as an importer of special fasteners and tools.

The company is organised around top-class sales-engineers with 25 to 30 years of experience accumulated in the fastener industry, specialised in producing and supplying quality hand and pneumatic tools alongside collated pins, nails and staples.

  • Manufacturer, exporter and importer under one roof
  • Precise, efficient manufacturing equipment and trained operators
  • Stringent quality control on every collation run
  • One-stop shopping at competitive price levels
More about YOU-ONE

Why YOU-ONE

Innovative expertise, applied to the whole line.

With the innovative spirit we develop the production line ourselves — collated nail machines for plastic sheet coil and gas-powered pins, collated screw machines and staple machines are all built in-house.
