About YOU-ONE

Established 2008. Built on 30 years in the fastener trade.

A Korean manufacturer and exporter of fasteners, fastening tools and equipment and fastener collating materials — and an importer of special fasteners and tools.

Our story

One partner for the fastener, the tool and the machine.

YOU-ONE Fastening Systems has been established as a Korean manufacturer and exporter of various fasteners, fastening tools & equipment and fastener collating materials, and as an importer of special fasteners and fastening tools, since the year 2008.

YOU-ONE is organised with top-class sales-engineers with over 25 to 30 years of experience accumulated in the fastener industry, and is specialised in producing and supplying quality hand and pneumatic tools as well as various kinds of collated pins, nails and staples.

We assure you that you can be served with tools and fasteners of top quality at a competitive price level from YOU-ONE, and that these products will lead to further success.

25–30Years of experience

Capabilities

Innovative expertise and experience in the fastening industry.

We support precise, efficient manufacturing equipment, well-trained employees and stringent quality control. With the innovative spirit, we develop the production line and build the collated nail, screw and staple machines ourselves.

Manufacturing

Collated pins, nails, staples, rings and screws produced on lines we specified and commissioned.

Machine building

Plastic sheet coil and gas-powered pin nail machines, screw machines and staple machines — in-house.

Quality control

Stringent inspection of wire, coating, collation strength and drive performance on every run.

Export & import

Export of our own production plus import of special fasteners and tools our customers ask for.

How we work

One-stop shopping, without the compromise.

Our top-quality product and excellent service, with one-stop shopping and competitive prices, make us the right solution for a nails and staples business.

  • Fasteners, tools and collating machinery from a single supplier
  • Samples and technical drawings before you commit to a run
  • Collation specs adapted to your existing nailers and magazines
  • OEM and private-label packaging on request
  • Direct line to sales-engineers, not a call centre
